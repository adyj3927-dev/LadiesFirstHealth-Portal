Women's Health Collective · UMass Amherst

“Healthcare is her right, not her reward.”

Ladies First advances reproductive and hormonal health equity for women at UMass Amherst. We provide community-driven education, peer support, and advocacy around conditions that go underdiagnosed and underserved, including PCOS and PMDD.

We work to raise awareness about underrecognized and underdiagnosed conditions, including Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) and Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder (PMDD), through community-driven education, peer support, and advocacy.

Our Initiatives

What we're building this year, from the classroom to the tabling table.

  • Health Education and Awareness

    • Raise awareness about PCOS, PMDD, and other underrecognized reproductive and hormonal health conditions.
    • Share accessible educational information with students.
    • Help address the dismissal and underdiagnosis of these conditions.

  • Health Navigation and Resources

    • Develop health navigation guides that point students toward doctors, testing, and support resources.
    • Provide educational materials that help students better understand their health.

  • Care Packages

    • Prepare care packages containing items such as a heating pad, PCOS and PMDD information guide, spearmint tea, and a supportive note.

  • Workshops and Panels

    • Organize educational sessions involving clinicians, researchers, and advocates with lived experience.

  • Peer Support and Community

    • Create opportunities for students to connect, share experiences, and support one another through peer support circles and community events.

  • Campus Outreach and Advocacy

    • Participate in campus events and tabling opportunities to raise awareness and connect with students.

  • Future Research Support

    • Explore opportunities to provide financial and in-kind support to research labs studying PCOS and PMDD.
